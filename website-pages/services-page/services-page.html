With each apartment

Focus on your studies, we'll handle the rest.

    Every apartment comes ready to move in, with all furniture, cutlery, linen, towels and bedding. Simply bring your luggage and begin your new chapter.

    Booking with us is like an extended hotel stay, with more competitive pricing, more space and your own kitchen. Amenities vary by building: some offer 24-hour reception, gyms and swimming pools.

    Housekeeping & maintenance

    All stays include housekeeping on a regular schedule, unless you opt out. We keep your place fresh, so your mind stays on your studies.

    Wi-Fi & utilities set up

    High-speed Wi-Fi, electricity, water and heating are all included and already running. Just unpack and connect.

With every booking

Perks for total comfort, flexibility and service.

    One price covering all bills, utilities and taxes. Some apartments ask for a security deposit, fully refundable at check-out provided the apartment is free of damage beyond fair wear and tear.

    Easy booking process

    One enquiry, a curated shortlist, and a simple, secure payment. Read our booking process and payment info in full.

    Impartial advice & guidance

    Unlike instant-booking platforms, we take the time to understand your needs before recommending a home. No admin fees, no hidden costs, and if we can't secure you a place, you won't pay a penny.

    Ongoing support

    A dedicated account manager, ongoing support and a 5-star guest experience. Some buildings add on-call maintenance, concierge and security teams.
