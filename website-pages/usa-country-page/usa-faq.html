Can international students rent an apartment in the USA without a US guarantor or credit history?

Yes. Standard US rentals usually ask for a US credit score, a social security number and a co-signer, which most international students don't have. None of our apartments require any of them. You secure your booking with a down payment and pay the rest in flexible instalments, more like booking a hotel than signing a lease.

Which US cities does Student Luxe cover?

Nine cities and growing: New York, Boston, Chicago, Washington DC, Los Angeles, Miami, New Orleans, Phoenix and Dallas, with Philadelphia coming soon. In every city we curate luxury buildings within easy reach of the major universities. Pick your city above to browse apartments.

Are the apartments furnished and are bills included?

Yes to both. Every apartment comes fully furnished with linen, towels and a fitted kitchen, so you arrive with just your luggage. Electricity, water and Wi-Fi are wrapped into one all-inclusive price, no utility accounts to set up and no surprise bills in a foreign currency.

Can I book for a semester, summer program or academic year?

Yes. Unlike standard US leases, which usually lock you into 12 months, our apartments are arranged around your course dates: a single semester, a summer program, an academic year or longer. Tell us your dates and we'll match you with buildings that fit.

How much does luxury student accommodation in the USA cost?

Luxury studios start from around $2,500 per month in cities like Phoenix, Dallas and New Orleans, rising to $5,000+ per month in Manhattan, always all-inclusive: rent, utilities and Wi-Fi in one figure, so you can compare cities like for like. Send an enquiry with your city and budget and we'll shortlist options for you.
