About our apartments

Furnished & professionally-managed residences

Through Student Luxe you can find and book luxury student apartments across the USA in New York, Boston, Washington DC, Chicago and Los Angeles, in prime, secure neighbourhoods within easy reach of the country's leading universities and colleges.

Our private student apartments in the USA are ideal for students (and parents) who are happy to invest a bit more for spacious, modern living - all in secure, prime locations, with all bills, Wi-Fi, housekeeping and resident support included as standard.

We are not a provider of shared student accommodation - ie. official university housing or dorms. If you are looking for your university's own on-campus housing, please check with their housing office directly.
