# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two audiences. Primary: Student Luxe guests, international students and their parents booking luxury serviced apartments in Central London (also Paris, New York, Cambridge, Madrid, Edinburgh). Wealthy, design-literate, often comparing against hotels and premium lettings. They receive documents (quotes, booking confirmations, payment plans) at emotionally significant moments: committing five figures to a year abroad. Secondary: the in-house marketing and bookings team (Alex, Marta, George, Alessandra) who build and send these documents from internal tools.

## Product Purpose

Squarespace site builds and guest-facing document design for studentluxe.co.uk: header/nav, page redesigns, and branded PDF documents (quotes, booking confirmations, payment plans) generated by internal Vercel tools. Success: every touchpoint reads as a boutique luxury service, not a student lettings agency; documents reassure at the moment of payment and feel worth keeping.

## Brand Personality

Quietly luxurious, editorial, personal. A knowledgeable concierge, not a booking platform. Warm confidence over corporate polish; restraint over flash. Serif-led typography with gold used as punctuation, never decoration for its own sake.

## Anti-references

- Corporate invoice: accounting-software energy, dense grey tables, terms-first layouts.
- Standard student housing: bright, youthful, purpose-built-student-accommodation branding (Unite Students, iQ), primary-colour blocks, emoji tone.
- Stuffy heritage: crests, flourishes, olde-worlde private-bank pastiche, fake wax seals.
- Generic SaaS document templates: hero metrics, icon card grids, gradient accents.

## Design Principles

1. Documents are keepsakes: design every PDF as something a guest might keep, not just file.
2. Serif carries the voice: Baskerville display with an italic gold accent word is the signature move.
3. Hairlines over boxes: structure with rules and whitespace before borders and cards.
4. One gold moment per surface: gold reads as punctuation; if everything is gold, nothing is.
5. Personal over institutional: name the guest, name the manager, write like a person.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Guest documents: print-safe contrast (ink on paper tones, no light-on-light), body copy at readable sizes (>=9.5pt in PDFs), information never conveyed by colour alone. Web builds: WCAG AA targets, honour prefers-reduced-motion, inputs >=16px on mobile per repo rules.
